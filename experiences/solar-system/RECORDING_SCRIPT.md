# Journey through the Solar System: narration recording script

Record each line as its own clip and name the file exactly as shown (MP3).
Tips: a quiet room, phone held about 20 cm away, speak slowly and warmly, like you're talking to a Grade 5 class.
Leave half a second of silence at the start and end. When the clips are ready, send them over and they'll be added to
`experiences/public/audio/solar-system/` with their names listed in `clips.json`.

| # | File | Say this |
|---|------|----------|
| 1 | `intro.mp3` | Welcome, space explorer! Today we will visit the Sun and all eight planets. Point at a button and squeeze the trigger, or pinch your fingers, to choose it. |
| 2 | `sun.mp3` | The Sun is a star: a giant ball of hot, glowing gas. Everything in the solar system goes around it. It is about 109 Earths wide, and it makes up almost all of the weight of the whole solar system. |
| 3 | `secret-sun.mp3` | The surface is around 5 500 °C, and light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes to reach Earth. |
| 4 | `mercury.mp3` | Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest. It has no air to hold heat in. Days are scorching hot and nights are freezing cold. A year here is only 88 Earth days long. |
| 5 | `secret-mercury.mp3` | Its surface is covered in craters from space rocks crashing into it, just like our Moon. |
| 6 | `venus.mp3` | Venus is wrapped in thick clouds that trap heat. That makes it the hottest planet, even hotter than Mercury. It also spins backwards compared to most planets. |
| 7 | `secret-venus.mp3` | It is about 465 °C on the surface: hot enough to melt lead! |
| 8 | `earth.mp3` | This is home! Earth is the only planet we know of with life. About 70% of it is covered in water. It takes 365 days to go around the Sun, which is one year. Earth has one Moon. |
| 9 | `secret-earth.mp3` | See the pin? That is Cape Town, where you are right now! The lights on the dark side are cities at night. |
| 10 | `mars.mp3` | Mars is called the Red Planet because its dust and rocks contain iron that has rusted. It has the biggest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons, and two tiny moons. |
| 11 | `secret-mars.mp3` | Robots called rovers are exploring Mars right now, looking for signs of water and life. |
| 12 | `jupiter.mp3` | Jupiter is the biggest planet. More than 1 000 Earths could fit inside it! It is a gas giant with no solid ground. The Great Red Spot is a storm bigger than Earth that has lasted for hundreds of years. |

| 14 | `saturn.mp3` | Saturn is famous for its beautiful rings. They are made of billions of pieces of ice and rock. Saturn is so light for its size that it would float in a giant bathtub of water! |

| 16 | `uranus.mp3` | Uranus is an ice giant that is tipped over on its side, so it rolls around the Sun like a ball. It is freezing cold, and one trip around the Sun takes 84 Earth years. |
