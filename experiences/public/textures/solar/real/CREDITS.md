# Planet maps (solar system, "real" set)

Brought over from the Beyond the Void prototype (Schools Experiences folder).

- Earth day, normal, specular (turned into `earth_rough`), city lights and clouds (inverted into `earth_clouds`):
  the three.js example textures, based on NASA Blue Marble / Black Marble imagery.
- Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune: Planet Pixel Emporium maps
  by James Hastings-Trew (planetpixelemporium.com), built from NASA/JPL mission imagery.
